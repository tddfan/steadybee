Year details & assumptions

Future pounds, not inflation-adjusted purchasing power. Return defaults are authored nominal total-return assumptions. Tax-excluded amounts use the whole figure; user deduction estimates are flat assumptions, not UK tax calculations. ISA and pension headroom use entered annual limits, not a legal eligibility check. Limits apply to model calendar years; tapering, MPAA, carry-forward, existing contributions elsewhere and statutory tax relief are not calculated. NI years add one assumed qualifying year for a year with salary; this does not establish entitlement. Quoted future budgets do not also receive inflation; today’s-price budgets grow with inflation. Employment salary uses active months and bonuses require employment in the payment month; other income/expenses and asset growth remain annual. The model starts January2026 and cannot show within-month shortages.

Withdrawals follow your selected owner/order and optional net ceiling; each account’s deduction assumption determines gross assets needed. Mortgage payoff respects the same account owner, order, deduction assumptions and annual withdrawal ceiling. Payroll employee/employer contributions follow employment and go to owned pots at year end; new contributions start earning the modelled return next year. Employee deductions are not repeated when already included in take-home. Salary sacrifice/net-pay options only change the base for a user deduction estimate; NI, statutory relief, matching and allowance limits are user-entered planning assumptions. Extra saving goals are funded only from income surplus. Main-home debt is linked to the home; rental income/costs are entered separately. Monthly home-mortgage interest/payment calculations assume a constant rate and annual cash funding. Named debt prepayments can include an entered fee and reduce payments or shorten the term; refinancing is not calculated. A vehicle purchase adds the funded portion to vehicle value.

The stress path lowers investment/pension/bond growth by two points, applies a 20% investment/pension loss (5% bonds) at Alex60 or later, and adds two points to expense growth. It is a declared scenario, not a probability estimate. Planning horizons are endpoints, not death predictions. Both people remain in the model through the shared horizon; survivor benefits, statutory tax relief, inheritance tax, vesting tax and death-benefit rules are not calculated. Remaining estate includes property. Saved copies last only for this page session; account connections and tracking remain planned.

Fictional illustration, not advice. Assumptions are editable; current rules are subject to change.
