How to read funding

Bars show income and withdrawals. The line shows spending.

How this illustration works

Future pounds

Figures are in future nominal pounds, not inflation-adjusted purchasing power. Return defaults represent authored total-return assumptions, not market forecasts.

Tax assumptions

Deduction estimates are user assumptions, not UK tax calculations. ISA and pension headroom use entered annual limits; tapering, MPAA, and statutory relief are not calculated.

Pay & pensions

Employment salary uses active months; bonuses require employment in the payment month. Employee and employer pension contributions go to owned pots at year end.

Stress scenario

The stress scenario lowers investment/pension growth by 2 points, applies a 20% market loss at Alex 60, and adds 2 points to spending inflation. These are illustrative scenarios, not probabilities.

Withdrawals & debt

Withdrawals follow selected owner order and annual net ceilings. Main-home debt is linked to the home; mortgages assume constant rate and annual cash funding.

Planning endpoints

Horizons are planning endpoints, not mortality predictions. Both individuals remain in the model through the horizon; survivor benefits and inheritance tax are not modelled.
