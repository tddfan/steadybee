Year details & assumptions

Future pounds, not inflation-adjusted purchasing power. Return defaults are authored nominal total-return assumptions. Tax-excluded amounts use the whole figure; user deduction estimates are flat assumptions, not UK tax calculations. ISA/pension/account labels do not calculate legal tax treatment. Quoted future budgets do not also receive inflation; today’s-price budgets grow with inflation. Employment salary uses active months and bonuses require employment in the payment month; other income/expenses and asset growth remain annual. The model starts January2026 and cannot show within-month shortages.

Withdrawals follow your selected owner/order and optional net ceiling; each account’s deduction assumption determines gross assets needed. Mortgage payoff respects the same account owner, order, deduction assumptions and annual withdrawal ceiling. Pension saving is funded only from income surplus. Main-home debt is linked to the home; rental income/costs are entered separately. Monthly home-mortgage interest/payment calculations assume a constant rate and annual cash funding. Fees, early repayment charges and refinancing are not calculated. A vehicle purchase adds the funded portion to vehicle value.

The stress path lowers investment/pension/bond growth by two points, applies a 20% investment/pension loss (5% bonds) at Alex60 or later, and adds two points to expense growth. It is a declared scenario, not a probability estimate. Planning horizons are endpoints, not death predictions. Both people remain in the model through the shared horizon; survivor benefits, statutory tax relief, inheritance tax, vesting tax and death-benefit rules are not calculated. Remaining estate includes property. Saved copies last only for this page session; account connections and tracking remain planned.
