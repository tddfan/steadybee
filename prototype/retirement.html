Year details & assumptions

Future pounds, not inflation-adjusted purchasing power. Asset rates are nominal net total-return assumptions, including dividends. Regular and one-off expenses grow with inflation plus their extra growth setting; lease and pension-saving rows use their declared growth only. Income grows at its own rate from the current year. Withdrawals use cash, fixed-income assets, investments and then accessible pension pots. This fixed withdrawal order is an illustration, not an optimised or tax-aware strategy. Pension saving shares surplus income in proportion to the entered goals; any unfunded saving goal is shown separately. The stress path reduces investment, pension and bond growth by two percentage points each year, applies a 20% trading/pension loss (5% for bonds) at Alex age 60 or the current age if later, and adds two points to expense growth. Debt interest and monthly payments are annualised; contractual payoff quotes, tax, fees beyond entered costs and within-year cash timing are not calculated.

Planning horizons are endpoints, not predicted death dates. Both people remain in the model through the shared horizon; survivor benefits, inheritance tax and death-benefit rules are not modelled. Remaining wealth includes property and is not an inheritance promise. Account connections, personal tax analysis and tracking are planned.
