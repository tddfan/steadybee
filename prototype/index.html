A few things to know.

Who is this for?

UK individuals and couples who want to organise their finances and explore illustrative retirement choices. It is an educational prototype, not personalised financial or tax advice.

Do I have to enter my own information?

No. Start with the editable fictional example. Your own picture is optional. No bank connection is required. You can explore without an account; signing in is only needed to save a cloud copy.

What gets saved?

Your household inputs and named scenarios can be saved to your signed-in account, then reopened on another device. Cloud storage is account-restricted but not end-to-end encrypted. You can also keep an encrypted device copy or download a passphrase-protected backup.

Can it tell me whether retirement will work?

It can illustrate how entered income and eligible assets might cover entered spending. Missing inputs and assumptions matter. Tax estimates are simplified, future rules can change, and the experimental simulation is not calibrated to your investment mix. Use the results to explore questions, not as a guarantee or a recommendation.
