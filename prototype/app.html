How this illustration works

The 2026–27 full rate is £241.30 a week. A simplified estimate uses projected qualifying years ÷ 35, capped at the full rate, with a 10-year minimum. Records before April 2016, contracting out, overseas years and protected amounts can change entitlement. Use an official forecast for your own amount.

Future qualifying years are an assumption of complete 12-month periods, not a check of your NI record. Income Tax uses recorded pay and the other taxable income you enter, under 2026–27 England/Wales/NI rules. No NI is deducted from State Pension. Other taxes and future changes are not modelled.
