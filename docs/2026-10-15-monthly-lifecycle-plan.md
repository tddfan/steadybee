# Monthly lifecycle prototype

Journey: assets and debt → income and spending across dates → planning assumptions.
Reuse the original lab's named life events and immediate response, without its authored pension amounts or portfolio claims.

1. Describe schedule statuses relative to the selected month; distinguish earlier phases from actual transaction history.
2. Provide named recurring, replacement spending, one-off income/cost and State Pension actions. Keep property values and mortgage details in assets; link the cash-flow rows back to their source.
3. Record State Pension qualifying years per person, assessment month, future qualifying-year assumption and pension start month. Offer a GOV.UK forecast or an explicitly simplified 35-year illustration. Do not infer a pension start date or NI record. Estimate incremental Income Tax from recorded pay plus an explicit other taxable income assumption; no NI on State Pension.
4. Apply the user's selected cash → own ISA → own general investment account order to each person's share of the remainder. Split the household recurring-spending reserve target by net income, including half shared income. Count owned cash plus the person's share of shared cash. No transfers between people or double-counted ISA headroom.
5. Verify deterministic calculations with tests, then exercise scheduling, editing and routes in the browser. These are single-month illustrations; no accumulated holdings or yearly ISA-reset projection is implied.
