# Inheritance: a simpler starting point

The main journey asks whose assets, how many children and (for couples) the relationship. A result is available immediately: balances after debts, Inheritance Tax, amount passing on, and each recipient's inheritance. Changing the number of children immediately applies an equal split and keeps matching child names.

New illustrations assume two children, deaths at approximately age 90, partner first then children, and a married/civil-partner relationship that is visible in the main selector. If both approximate death dates match, the second is moved one year later; the actual assumed ages are shown. The main home is assumed eligible for the home allowance, UK scope and standard DC funds are assumed, and pension nominations follow the recipient split. These are modelling assumptions, not verified facts or a forecast of anyone's death.

Today’s balances are the starting basis. The main view states they stay fixed with no future spending or growth. Optional Planning balances use the first-death month, then stay fixed between deaths. A future death date selects the relevant pension tax rules; it does not grow today’s balances. A short pension-beneficiary Income Tax exclusion stays beside the result.

Change assumptions exposes dates, routes, home eligibility, scope, pension treatment and exceptional items. Names and custom shares have a separate disclosure next to the recipient amounts. Assets, ownership, estate stages and detailed limitations are secondary. The information icon still offers the numerical calculation and CSV for Excel. Existing customised saved settings remain intact; only the exact untouched old empty scaffold upgrades to the new starting illustration.

The underlying estate calculator and thresholds are unchanged. New tests cover usable defaults, individual and couple death-date assumptions, distribution reconciliation and original-data immutability. Inheritance, saved-household and deployment checks pass (33 tests). Browser verification uses only the synthetic sample: changing children and relationship, switching to Planning balances, both inheritance routes, individual estates, calculation dialog and narrow mobile layouts.

References checked 24 October 2026: [GOV.UK Inheritance Tax](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax) and [pensions technical note](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/inheritance-tax-on-pensions-technical-note/technical-note-inheritance-tax-on-pensions).
