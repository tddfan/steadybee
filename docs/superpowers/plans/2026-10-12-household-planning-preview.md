# Household planning preview

Approved direction: replace the retirement landing demo with three editable stages: household assets/debts; dated income/spending; a scenario planner with wealth and funding-source graphs. Preserve the warm app theme and icon-led composition. Keep the original planner and ledger fixtures intact.

Build a separate framework-free fictional household ledger. Include asset growth, debt interest/payments, owner-specific pension access/work dates, multiple income/expense streams, one-offs, funded pension saving, scenario combinations, downsizing and investment transfers. Show net wealth separately from accessible assets. Investment sales/dividends release asset money rather than add fictitious income. Entered incomes are net assumed; no tax calculation. Defaults are authored illustrations, not fetched market rates or official pension entitlements.

Use annual nominal flows and clearly label future pounds. Apply each configured growth rate. State Pension income/start dates and inflation are editable. Show a deterministic baseline, selected scenario and declared stress path; do not invent probability bands. Planning horizons do not infer death or model survivor benefits. Unsupported tax/estate/automatic tracking remains planned.

Fast numeric inputs, frequency selectors, owner/date ranges, optional growth, duplicate/add/remove rows. Simulations run from confirmed fictional figures; nothing is stored or connected. Meaningful ledger tests first, then responsive browser checks, build, existing tests and commit/push only codex/retirement-landing.

Completed:
- Today has editable icon cards for savings, trading/ISAs, each pension, home, car and fixed-income assets; net wealth and accessible assets stay separate. Debt balances, annual interest and monthly payments are editable, with add/remove support.
- Income/spending supports multiple owned streams, monthly/yearly conversion, copy/remove/add, age-range sliders and growth, plus dated one-offs and pension-saving goals. State Pension ages/amounts are fictional editable inputs. Saving goals that exceed income surplus are visible.
- Plan shows simultaneous wealth and income/funding graphs, work-end and horizon controls for both people, annual inspection and fourteen combinable actions. The income graph includes surplus earnings, internal distributions/sales and cash mortgage repayment; those cash flows do not create wealth. Pension saving is allocated to owners in proportion to funded goals.
- Wealth includes property/debt; funding never automatically spends home equity or locked pensions. The stress path has explicitly declared return, loss and cost settings; no stochastic probabilities, tax optimisation, official entitlement checking or survivor/inheritance calculation is claimed.
