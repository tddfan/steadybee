# Steadybee — compact landing-page proposal

11 October 2026 · Draft for founder review

The page layout and click behaviour are specified in [LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md](LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md).

## Purpose

Make the retirement purpose immediately clear, explain the experience in plain language, and let visitors start with a relevant question. This copy describes the intended product; it is not a claim that the current research prototype calculates personal retirement outcomes or connects accounts.

## Recommended hero copy

Headline: **When could you retire—and what could you live on?**

Supporting copy: See your retirement picture. Explore what changes it. Track your progress as life changes.

Primary action: **Start my free retirement plan**

Secondary action: **See Alex and Jemma's example**

Supporting line: Free to start. No account connection needed.

The personal-plan action is proposed production copy. The current prototype must describe starting a draft rather than suggest that it delivers a calculated personal forecast. Example results must be clearly identified as fictional and generated by verified calculation code.

## Visual explanation below the hero

Labelled pensions, savings and investment icons converge into one retirement plan, with income, spending and commitments completing the picture. One example panel demonstrates three stages using the same household and graph.

**Build your retirement picture**
See when your plan covers your spending and where it falls short, with a retirement timeline and inspectable assumptions.

**Explore what changes**
Compare retiring earlier, losing income, spending differently or a market setback. One active slider or shortcut updates the graph and its explanation.

**Follow your plan**
Compare the dated plan with actual experience and the latest forecast. Distinguish chosen annual actions, completed changes and market movements.

## Exploration prompts

Introduce them with: **What would you like to explore?**

- When could I retire?
- What if one of us stops earning?
- What if we spend less or save more?
- What if our investments fall?

In the landing example, these prompts select a supported comparison for Alex and Jemma. Starting a personal plan opens the same questions using the visitor's own household context. Keep the first view focused on one clear primary action.

## Compact free and paid explanation

**Start free:** build your plan, explore basic scenarios and update your figures yourself.

**Go deeper when you need to:** compare more complex choices, understand investment exposure and tax effects, and add automatic updates from supported accounts.

People can use paid comparisons with manually entered figures. Connections are optional. Paid previews explain the question, scope and key trade-offs; existing results remain understandable without purchasing an explanation.

The proposed £5-per-household monthly price remains a hypothesis. If adopted, explain it here with a clear feature comparison rather than crowding the hero.

## Progressive detail

After visitors understand the core experience, explain holdings analysis, spending tracking, tax-aware annual choices, housing and family scenarios. Describe each through the retirement question it helps answer. State supported account coverage where connections are offered.

Alex and Jemma's visitor-controlled fictional walkthrough covers their situation, retirement picture, choices and later review. Author the numerical baseline and capture the screens after the relevant app features work. Any figures need reproducible workings; this draft does not invent demo amounts, testimonials, usage counts or certainty claims.

Use one qualified DWP research fact as supporting evidence below the question; its wording and source are recorded in [LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md](LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md). It measures retirement income understanding, not awareness of Steadybee.

Keep assumptions and missing information inspectable beside results. Show factual privacy and data controls only when they are implemented and verified. Plain language, readable type, keyboard access and generous targets remain required.

## Comprehension test

Show the first view to people in the intended audience and ask them to explain what the product does, who it is for and what they expect after clicking. Check whether retirement, household planning, scenario exploration and continuing tracking are understood. Then observe whether they choose a relevant starting question and complete the next step. No claim that this hook converts better has been established.
